Pioneering India's School of Insurance Studies

How Shri Kailash Nath Bhandari conceptualized and established India’s first multidisciplinary centre for insurance law, actuarial risk analysis, and underwriting management at National Law University, Jodhpur.

The Founding Imperative • 2004

Bridging Jurisprudence and Underwriting

Following the opening of India’s insurance sector to private and foreign participation under IRDAI in 2000, a critical systemic vulnerability emerged: India possessed brilliant lawyers and skilled accountants, but virtually no professionals trained in the intricate intersection of statutory insurance law and commercial underwriting.

Having stepped down from the CMD leadership of The New India Assurance, Shri Bhandari brought his proposal to the leadership of National Law University, Jodhpur. He envisioned not a vocational certificate course, but a rigorous, full-fledged university school: the School of Insurance Studies (SIS), now known as the Centre for Insurance Studies and Research (CIS).

As Founder Honorary Director, Mr. Bhandari formulated the foundational curriculum, recruited leading industry practitioners as guest faculty, established scholarships, and secured direct placement pathways with GIC Re, public insurers, private underwriters, and international reinsurance brokers.

Founder Honorary Director's Message
“Insurance without legal exactitude is gambling; law without commercial underwriting knowledge is sterile doctrine. At NLU Jodhpur, we created the crucible where both disciplines unite to fortify the nation’s economic backbone.”
— Prof. K. N. Bhandari
