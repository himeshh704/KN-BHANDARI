Keynote Address • Mumbai, 2001 REF: KNB-SP-2001-01

The Sovereign Covenant: Fiduciary Vigilance in National Non-Life Underwriting

Delivered before the Annual Convocation of the Insurance Institute of India

“When an insurance certificate is issued by a sovereign corporation, it is not merely a commercial agreement; it is a solemn promise guaranteed by the Republic that in catastrophe, economic ruin will not be allowed to triumph over human endeavor.”

In this seminal address, Shri Bhandari outlined why public sector insurance companies must maintain rigorous catastrophic reserves and technical solvency rather than engaging in destructive price-war discounting during market deregulation.

Corporate Governance Essay • 2018 REF: KNB-ESSAY-2018

Independence as Conscience: The Modern Director's Duty to the Voiceless Shareholder

National Symposium on Boardroom Stewardship & Company Law Reforms

“The independent director does not attend board meetings to collect sitting fees or exchange pleasantries with industrialists. They are there as an uncompromised sentinel for the retired pensioner holding five hundred shares.”

Drawing upon eighteen years on the board of Hindalco Industries and public corporations, Shri Bhandari articulates the precise mechanics of audit committee oversight, related-party transaction scrutiny, and dissenting fiduciary votes.

Jodhpur Heritage Address • 2012 REF: KNB-JOD-2012

From Sardar School to the Boardroom: What Marwari Thrift & Integrity Taught Me

Address to the Students & Faculty of Sardar School & Jodhpur University Alumni

“The dust of Jodhpur teaches you two indelible truths: that water must never be wasted, and that a spoken word of promise is more binding than ten legal contracts.”

An inspiring memoir-style address connecting the frugal, disciplined, and community-centric upbringing of 1950s Jodhpur with the complex corporate governance demands of 21st-century global flagships.
