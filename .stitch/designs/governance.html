Mr. Bhandari served as an Independent Director on the Board of Hindalco Industries Limited for eighteen transformative years. In strict compliance with Section 149(11) of the Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI Listing Regulations, his tenure concluded upon the completion of his second consecutive five-year term on 29 August 2024.

Throughout this decisive era of global expansion—including the landmark cross-border acquisition and governance integration of Novelis Inc.—Mr. Bhandari provided institutional leadership, spearheading internal audit governance and fiduciary accountability.

PERSPECTIVES FROM THE BOARDROOM

The Role of the Independent Director in Modern Indian Industry

"The independent director exists neither to obstruct executive ambition nor to rubber-stamp management decree. Their solitary allegiance is to statutory integrity, institutional perpetuity, and the voiceless minority shareholder."

— K. N. Bhandari, Notes on Boardroom Conduct & Fiduciary Law (2018)

Constructive Skepticism

A duty to question commercial assumptions, review capital expenditure allocations, and insist on transparent risk mitigation strategies prior to board ratification.

Statutory Sanctity

Uncompromising conformity to the Companies Act, SEBI LODR, and sector regulators, prioritizing legal clarity and formal disclosure over expediency.
