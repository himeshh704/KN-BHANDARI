Academic Institution

School of Insurance Studies, NLU Jodhpur

Founder Honorary Director

Recognizing that India's expanding non-life financial sector required legal professionals with deep grounding in technical insurance practice, Mr. Bhandari partnered with National Law University, Jodhpur to establish the School of Insurance Studies (SIS / Center for Insurance Studies).

Serving as its Founder Honorary Director, he architected postgraduate and specialized curricula uniting contractual jurisprudence with underwriting discipline, reinsurance treaties, and claims adjudication.

Community Heritage

Preserving Educational & Civic Foundations in Marwar

Civic Custodianship

President, Shri Oswal Singh Sabha

Sardar School & Sardar Doon Public School Campus

Deeply rooted in his native Marwar, Mr. Bhandari served as President of Shri Oswal Singh Sabha, one of Jodhpur's premier historic community organizations. Under his guidance, the Sabha stewarded the venerable 125-year Sardar School and Sardar Doon Public School campus in Sardarpura.

His leadership preserved heritage sandstone infrastructure while expanding modern science laboratories, digital learning tools, and inclusive sports facilities for thousands of students.
